# Project Charter
## Smart School Management System (Smart SMS)

**Project Name:** Smart SMS
**Sponsor:** School Director (Ligemir/Smart Private School)
**Project Manager:** Werkyantfu
**Date:** October 2026

---

## 1. Project Purpose
The purpose of this project is to design and develop a comprehensive web-based School Management System for a Private School (KG – Grade 12). The system will automate administrative tasks, digitize student records, streamline fee collection, and improve communication between the school and parents.

## 2. Objectives
- **Digitize 100%** of student and staff records by the end of Phase 1.
- **Automate fee collection** and invoicing to reduce financial tracking errors.
- **Enable parents** to view their child's academic progress and fee status online.
- **Reduce administrative workload** for teachers and the registrar.

## 3. Scope (High-Level)
- **In-Scope:** SIS, Finance, LMS, HR/Payroll, Inventory, Transport, Communication, Analytics.
- **Out-of-Scope:** School website, library management system (will be added in future updates).

## 4. Key Milestones
| Milestone | Target Date | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Project Kick-off | Week 1 | Team alignment & setup |
| Phase 1 Complete | Week 4 | Auth, DB, & SIS Module |
| Phase 2 Complete | Week 8 | Finance, LMS, & HR Modules |
| Phase 3 Complete | Week 11 | Ops, Transport & Analytics |
| Go-Live (Launch) | Week 12 | Official Handover |

## 5. Success Criteria
- The system must be adopted by 100% of school staff (Registrar, Accountants, Teachers).
- 0 data loss during initial data migration.
- Positive feedback from parents using the portal.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Sponsor (Director):** Approves budget and final sign-off.
- **Project Manager (Werkyantfu):** Oversees development, ensures deadlines are met.
- **Registrar / Accountant:** Provide testing feedback (UAT).
- **IT Support:** Maintains the server post-launch.
