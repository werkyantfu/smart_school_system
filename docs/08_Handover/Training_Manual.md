# Training Manual
## Smart SMS Rollout Plan

To ensure a smooth transition, the school staff will be trained in 4 sessions.

## Day 1: Administrators & Registrar
- How to log in.
- How to register new students (KG - Grade 12).
- How to generate reports.

## Day 2: Finance & Accountants
- How to create fee invoices.
- How to record payments (Cash, Telebirr, CBE).
- How to print receipts.

## Day 3: Teachers
- How to enter exam marks.
- How to upload assignments.
- How to view class schedules.

## Day 4: Parents (Orientation)
- How to log in to the Parent Portal.
- How to view report cards.
- How to pay fees online.
