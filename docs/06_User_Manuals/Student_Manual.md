# Student Manual
## Smart School Management System (Smart SMS)

**For:** Students (Grade 1 – Grade 12)
**Author:** Werkyantfu
**Date:** October 2026

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Smart SMS! This guide will show you how to log in and use the system to check your grades, view your class schedule, and download assignments.

---

## 2. How to Log In (እንዴት እንደሚገቡ)
1. Open your web browser (Chrome or Firefox).
2. Go to the school's Smart SMS website (e.g., `http://localhost:5173`).
3. Enter your **Student ID** and **Password** provided by the school.
4. Click the **"Login"** button.

*(Note: If you forget your password, please contact the school Registrar).*

---

## 3. Understanding Your Dashboard (የመጀመሪያ ገጽዎ)
Once you log in, you will see your dashboard. Here you can find:
- **My Profile:** Your personal information.
- **My Grades:** Your exam results.
- **Assignments:** Homework and notes from teachers.
- **Schedule:** Your daily class timetable.

---

## 4. How to Check Your Grades (ውጤትዎን እንዴት ማየት እንደሚችሉ)
1. Click on **"My Grades"** from the side menu.
2. Select the **Term** (e.g., Term 1).
3. You will see a list of subjects with your scores for quizzes, midterms, and final exams.
4. Click **"Download Report Card"** to save a PDF copy.

---

## 5. How to Download Assignments (የቤት ስራ እንዴት ማውረድ እንደሚችሉ)
1. Click on **"Assignments"** from the menu.
2. You will see a list of tasks from your teachers.
3. Click the **"Download"** button next to the file to save it to your computer or phone.

---

## 6. Need Help? (እርዳታ ከፈለጉ)
If you have any problems using the system, please ask your homeroom teacher or visit the school IT office.
